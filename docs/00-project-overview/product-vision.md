# Product Vision

> Status: Draft (preserves the original vision) · Last updated: 2026-11-09
> Derived from `docs/09-project-history/original-planning-document-v1.0.md` §1.

## 1. Vision statement

"Help everyone discover their best hairstyle through accessible, realistic, and intelligent
virtual experiences."

## 2. Problem

People struggle to predict how a hairstyle will look on their own face. Photos of models do not
transfer to their features, the risk of a bad haircut is real, and a barber consultation does not
always include a visual preview. Trial and error is slow and expensive.

## 3. Product goals

1. Let users try hairstyles in real time through a browser camera.
2. Support a range of face shapes, skin tones, and hair characteristics.
3. Provide an organized, filterable hairstyle catalog.
4. Help users shortlist styles they like.
5. Keep the experience usable on mid-range mobile devices.
6. Provide a foundation for a broader AI personal-appearance platform.

## 4. Target users

- Men and women considering a new hairstyle.
- People collecting reference images before visiting a barber or salon.
- Users who want a quick, private, no-install preview.
- Future: barbers and stylists using the tool during consultations.

## 5. Product principles

1. Real-time first.
2. Privacy first: local processing, explicit consent, no silent uploads.
3. Simple and intuitive interface.
4. Realistic visualization over novelty effects.
5. Modular, testable architecture.
6. Mobile performance is a requirement, not an afterthought.
7. Recommendations must be explainable and framed as suggestions, never judgments.

## 6. Success signals (MVP)

These are product signals, not verified metrics. Targets are defined in the PRD and benchmark plan.

- A first-time user can enable the camera and see a hairstyle without reading instructions.
- Tracking remains visually stable during normal head movement.
- A user can switch styles without restarting the camera.
- A user can save a snapshot they would plausibly show a stylist.
- The app runs on a mid-range Android phone at a usable frame rate.

## 7. What success does not mean

- Photorealistic hair replacement.
- Perfect occlusion of existing hair.
- Accurate hairstyle advice from a single image.
- A full fashion or beauty platform.

## 8. Explicit non-goals for the MVP

Clothing, pants, shoes, bags, accessories, accounts, cloud storage, social sharing, payments,
marketplace, and barber booking. See `docs/01-product/product-scope.md`.

## 9. Change control

This vision is preserved unless the user explicitly approves a change. Changes require:

1. An updated PRD or scope document.
2. A decision-log entry.
3. User approval.
